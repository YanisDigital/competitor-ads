# Short pitch for the client or a project reply (template)

Length: 8-12 lines. Only checked numbers from the snapshot. No promises of results, no invented cases.

1. **What I looked at.** Niche, place, date, N competitors after cleaning (and that the list was confirmed), N ads.
2. **Three observations**, each with a number and a link to one ad:
   - what almost everyone does;
   - what the longest-running ads do;
   - what nobody says (a free angle, not circular).
3. **What I would test first.** Two hypotheses from test_plan.json, one line each, with the metric.
4. **What I need from you.** 3-5 items from `confirm_with_client` (prices, terms, proof, last order date, who answers leads).
5. **How we work.** Conversion channel and budget stated as assumptions ("per campaign" or "in total"), review after the first round.

Do not send the report with the pitch unless the client asked: it holds third-party ad texts and notes for the targeting specialist.
